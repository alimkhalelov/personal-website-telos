# Fan-Filter-Scale Methodology

The **Fan-Filter-Scale (FFS)** methodology is an AI-native operating model designed for modern Product Managers and Demiurges to discover product-market fit at machine speed while eliminating wasted engineering cycles.

---

## 1. Phase 1: Fan (Divergent Ideation & Parallel Spawning)

Instead of committing engineering resources to a single monolithic roadmap item, the team spawns a wide fan of lightweight, isolated experiment vectors:

- **AI Multi-Variant Generation**: Leveraging [[systems/demiurge-os|Demiurge.OS]] and AI subagent swarms to synthesize user pains into 10–20 distinct problem/solution hypotheses simultaneously.
- **Autonomous Landing Pipelines**: Automated generation of landing pages, value propositions, and interactive prototypes with minimal code footprint.
- **Zero-Friction Distribution**: Micro-campaigns across targeted channels to gather raw user interaction metrics.

---

## 2. Phase 2: Filter (Signal Extraction & ICE Pruning)

The Filter phase acts as an objective algorithmic gateway, filtering out weak signals and cognitive biases:

- **Quantitative Thresholds**: Conversion rates, click-through signals, and qualitative feedback loops are monitored against strict baseline thresholds.
- **ICE / Mom-Test Scoring**: Automated customer discovery loops ground hypotheses in verified customer behavior rather than polite compliments.
- **Sunk Cost Annihilation**: Unsuccessful experiment nodes are pruned immediately without emotional attachment.

---

## 3. Phase 3: Scale (Vertical Deepening & Autonomous Loops)

When an experiment vector passes the Filter gate with statistically significant retention signals, it advances to Phase 3:

- **Loop Engineering Integration**: Implementation of [[concepts/loop-engineering|Loop Engineering]] to construct durable, autonomous loops around core product mechanics.
- **Knowledge Graph Grounding**: Cross-linking system entities into [[concepts/graphrag-knowledge|GraphRAG]] to maintain architectural integrity and prevent recency bias.
- **Generative Distribution**: Applying [[concepts/geo-and-aeo|GEO & AEO]] strategies to dominate LLM search engine citations and organic discovery.
