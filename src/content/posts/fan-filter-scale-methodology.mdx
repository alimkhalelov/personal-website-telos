The **Fan-Filter-Scale** methodology is the core engine behind building high-leverage products that can scale autonomously. As an AI-Native Product Manager, I've developed this approach to systematically test, refine, and expand product ideas with minimal overhead.

## 1. Fan
The **Fan** stage is all about divergence. We generate a wide array of hypotheses, rapid prototypes, and potential features. In this phase, quantity leads to quality. Leveraging AI agents allows us to multiply our creative output, exploring hundreds of potential product angles simultaneously without the traditional cost in time or resources.

## 2. Filter
Once we have a broad spectrum of ideas, the **Filter** stage applies strict, data-driven constraints. We ruthlessly eliminate what doesn't work, focusing on user engagement, technical feasibility, and value creation. This is where the "noise" is stripped away, leaving only the strongest, most viable signals.

## 3. Scale
The final stage is **Scale**. Having identified the winning hypotheses, we double down. We build robust, autonomous systems around these core features, automating the delivery of value. The goal is to reach a state where the product can grow and adapt with minimal human intervention—acting as a true Demiurge of its domain.

By continuously iterating through this loop, we can build products that are not just fast to market, but highly resilient and scalable.
