# Ocus — EU Crypto Compliance, Banking & Operations OS

**Ocus** is an institutional operating system that converts over 1,000 pages of EU regulatory directives (MiCA, DORA, DAC8) into an actionable, deterministic 31-node operations DAG paired with an interactive operational launch cockpit. Pre-integrated with Fireblocks custody, Cobo MPC, Clear Junction fiat rails, and Tier-1 European credit institutions.

## Core Architectural Pillars

### 1. Directive Ingestion & Atomization
Parses complex legal articles from EU MiCA, DORA, and DAC8 directives into atomic, falsifiable requirements. Each requirement is mapped to concrete inputs, technical checks, and evidence gates anchored to ESMA supervisory standards.

### 2. Operational DAG Synthesis & Critical Path Engine
Synthesizes a 31-node Directed Acyclic Graph across corporate entity structuring, capital tests, safeguarding accounts, and MPC key management. Employs topological sorting to calculate the mathematical critical path, targeting production go-live in 15 weeks.

### 3. Interactive Launch Cockpit & 15-Week Gantt Schedule
Hardware-accelerated SVG interactive node topology with dynamic zoom, panning, and a real-time readiness gauge (48% target milestone). Includes a 15-week execution schedule mapping critical path milestones to supervisory interview dates.

### 4. Pre-Wired Banking & Custodial Rails
Eliminates up to 6 months of institutional banking friction through pre-formatted safeguarding packs, AML compliance manuals, and technical architecture dossiers validated by Tier-1 European legal firms.

### 5. Continuous Supervisory Telemetry & Rule Drift Alerts
Maintains live synchronization with ESMA supervisory registers, tracks capital sufficiency against regulatory minimums (€350,000 floor vs. €500,000 buffer), and alerts compliance teams to upcoming regulatory technical standards before enforcement.
