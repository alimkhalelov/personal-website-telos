# Nebuly — Weekly AI Brief & Stakeholder Enablement Platform

**Nebuly** is an executive intelligence platform for enterprise AI adoption that bridges high-level C-suite narrative storytelling with granular OHLC conversation telemetry, interactive three-order consequence simulation, and guardrailed enablement kits for cross-functional teams.

[![Live Platform](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live%20Platform-alimkhalelov.github.io%2Fnebuly-354C46?style=flat-square)](https://alimkhalelov.github.io/nebuly/)
[![App Store Showcase](https://img.shields.io/badge/App%20Store%20Card-%2Fprojects%2Fnebuly-10b981?style=flat-square)](https://alim.dest.page/projects/nebuly)

## Core Architectural Pillars

### 1. Launch Film Storytelling & Executive Narrative
Pairs cinematic video storytelling with weekly operational briefings to align C-suite stakeholders, engineering leads, and business unit heads on verifiable AI ROI rather than speculative hype.

### 2. OHLC Financial-Grade Conversation Telemetry
Translates raw model usage logs and token telemetry into Open-High-Low-Close financial candlesticks, mapping conversation costs, latency curves, and resolution efficiency against user frustration thresholds.

### 3. Three-Order Consequence Cascade
Interactive dependency simulator modeling ripple effects across enterprise AI decisions:
- **1st-Order:** Model routing and latency impacts
- **2nd-Order:** Cost redistribution and prompt coaching efficiencies
- **3rd-Order:** Cross-functional adoption and organizational AI literacy

### 4. Scroll-Synced Sticky Triage & Enablement Kits
Sticky chapter indicator synchronized with page scroll depth, allowing leadership to check off operational triage actions and immediately export guardrailed deployment templates for cross-functional execution.
