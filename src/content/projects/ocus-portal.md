# Ocus Portal — Interactive Operations, Launch Map & Rules Engine

**Ocus Portal** is an interactive operational launch cockpit for Web3 institutions, crypto brokers, and stablecoin issuers navigating European regulatory approval.

[![Live Portal Cockpit](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live%20Cockpit-ocus--eu.vercel.app%2Focus-38bdf8?style=flat-square)](https://ocus-eu.vercel.app/ocus)

## Core Architectural Pillars

### 1. Interactive Launch Map Graph Canvas
Hardware-accelerated SVG interactive node topology with dynamic zoom, panning, and category clustering across Entity, Capital, Tech, Safeguarding, and Legal domains. Features a real-time readiness dial calculating overall progress toward the 48% launch milestone.

### 2. 15-Week Gantt Execution Schedule
Phase-by-phase timeline mapping critical path milestones from initial incorporation through safeguarding account opening to final supervisory interview and cross-border passporting.

### 3. Capital & Financial Modeler
Dynamic simulation engine modeling regulatory capital floors (€350,000) against maintained reserves (€500,000), monthly compliance burn rates, and safeguarding volume fees. Generates exportable financial viability packs for supervisory review.

### 4. Regulatory Drift & Rule Changes Tracker
Automated diff engine continuously monitoring updates to ESMA Regulatory Technical Standards (RTS) and European Banking Authority (EBA) guidelines, automatically highlighting affected nodes across active operational tracks.
