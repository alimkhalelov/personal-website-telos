# Pet-Projects Labs: The Experimental Incubator

**Pet-Projects Labs** is an open incubation gallery and testing ground for AI-driven interfaces, autonomous micro-agents, and experimental web interactions.

## Focus Areas

1. **Autonomous Agents & Micro-Bots**
   - Rapid development of single-purpose task agents running on structured ReAct loops.

2. **Interface Prototypes & Avant-Garde UX**
   - Exploring non-standard human-AI interaction paradigms: zero-click interfaces, ambient cognitive dashboards, and tactile solid-pod architectures.

3. **Open Research & Public Benchmarks**
   - Real-world telemetry on model latencies, reasoning architectures, and prompt-to-code velocity.
