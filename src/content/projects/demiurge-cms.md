# Demiurge CMS & AI Drafting Studio

**Demiurge CMS** is a next-generation editorial platform built for high-velocity technical writing, product thinking, and multi-channel publication.

## Core Capabilities

1. **Interactive AI Interviewer ('Grill-Me')**
   - Conducts structured, one-question-at-a-time technical interviews to extract authentic domain depth and user intent before generating draft content.

2. **Anti-Slop Humanization & Tone Calibration**
   - Strips generic corporate cliches, empty fluff, and AI-sounding patterns to preserve sharp, opinionated, first-principles writing.

3. **Multi-Channel Syndication Engine**
   - Automatically formats and publishes technical insights to Telegram, X/Twitter threads, and LinkedIn on publication.
