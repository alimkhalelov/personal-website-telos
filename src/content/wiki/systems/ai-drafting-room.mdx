# AI Drafting Room Engine

The **AI Drafting Room** (`/admin/draft`) is a bespoke content studio that accelerates article drafting from raw scattered ideas to polished, publication-ready essays.

## Key Capabilities

1. **Grill-Me Flow**: An interactive interviewer agent that interrogates the author to extract non-obvious insights and resolve architectural ambiguities.
2. **Multi-Model Intelligence**: Dynamically falls back across Gemini 3.1 Pro, 3.5 Flash, 3.1 Flash-Lite, and OpenAI models.
3. **Stream Protocol Invariants**: Server streams via `toUIMessageStreamResponse()` to avoid 500 timeouts on Vercel serverless execution.
4. **Rich-Text & Markdown**: Dual-mode Tiptap visual editor with direct AST synchronization.

## Relationship to Sovereign Vault

Once an article is finalized in the drafting room, it can be committed directly to `src/content/posts` or compiled into the [[systems/category-rag|CategoryRAG Knowledge Core]] as a sovereign wiki node.
