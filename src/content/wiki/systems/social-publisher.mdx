# Social Publisher Engine

The **Social Publisher** (`src/lib/social-publisher.ts`) eliminates manual distribution overhead by transforming long-form MDX posts into tailored social micro-content.

## Channel Adapters

- **Telegram Channel Bot**: Formats markdown into clean HTML with preview cards.
- **Twitter/X API v2**: Generates threaded tweets with optimal hook/body/CTA pacing.
- **LinkedIn REST API**: Produces executive summary posts with formatted bullet points.

## Integration in Demiurge.OS

Triggered automatically upon blog publish or via `/api/admin/publish` webhook, forming the final step in the [[concepts/fan-filter-scale|Fan-Filter-Scale]] distribution loop.
