## TL;DR
**Project Telos** represents the long-term vision of an autonomous operating system for digital creators and builders. It serves as the parent framework for the experiments showcased in [Pet-Projects Labs](/petprojects).

---

## The Labs Ecosystem
1. **Agentic Workspaces**: Sandboxed environments where LLM loops test autonomous workflows.
2. **Interactive Prototypes**: Fast MVP experiments testing the limits of human-agent collaboration and zero-click onboarding.
3. **Pixel Art & Aesthetics**: Harmonizing high-tech autonomous systems with nostalgic and playful visual artifacts (such as the Pet-Projects pixel mascot).
