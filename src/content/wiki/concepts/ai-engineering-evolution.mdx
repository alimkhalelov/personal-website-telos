# AI Engineering Evolution & Vibecoding

The arrival of frontier reasoning models marks the shift from manual token-by-token coding to **Vibecoding & System Architecture**.

## The Hierarchy of AI Engineering

1. **Level 1 (Assisted Coding)**: Tab autocomplete and single-line generation.
2. **Level 2 (Chat-Driven Coding)**: Multi-turn prompt-response iterations.
3. **Level 3 (Autonomous Loop Engineering)**: Agent harnesses executing plans, running tests, and recovering from errors via [[concepts/loop-engineering|Loop Engineering]].
4. **Level 4 (Living Demiurge OS)**: Multi-agent ecosystem self-maintaining knowledge graphs, deployment pipelines, and live feedback loops as embodied in [[entities/demiurge-os|Demiurge.OS]].
