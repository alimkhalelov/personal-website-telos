# GraphRAG: Cosmic Knowledge Retrieval

**GraphRAG** combines graph databases and abstract syntax trees (ASTs) with vector retrieval to overcome the fundamental limitations of naive text chunking.

## Why Flat Vector RAG Fails

Traditional vector search splits documents into fixed-size chunks (e.g. 500 tokens). This destroys:
- Global entity relationships across documents.
- Strict hierarchical inheritance.
- Non-linear dependencies between architectural invariants.

## The GraphRAG Advantage

By constructing an interconnected entity-relationship graph, GraphRAG enables:
- **Deterministic Traversal**: Multi-hop reasoning without context window blowout.
- **Topological Clustering**: Understanding high-level domain boundaries.
- **Zero Hallucinations**: Enforced by [[systems/category-rag|CategoryRAG]], where facts must resolve to verifiable graph nodes.
