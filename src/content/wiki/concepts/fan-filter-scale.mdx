# Fan-Filter-Scale Methodology

The **Fan-Filter-Scale** framework is a high-leverage product creation methodology designed by [[entities/alimzhan-khalelov|Alimzhan Khalelov]]. It replaces linear waterfall product development with an asymmetric algorithmic pipeline.

## The Three Phases

### 1. Fan (Divergent Ideation & Signal Gathering)
Generate a wide array of hypotheses from uncurated market signals, customer friction points, and edge-case anomalies. We avoid premature optimization at this stage and encourage high variance.

### 2. Filter (Brutal Quantitative & Qualitative Stress-Testing)
Subject every hypothesis to rigorous automated evaluators, Mom Tests, cold customer interviews, and ICE (Impact, Confidence, Ease) scoring. Over 90% of ideas are discarded here with near-zero sunk cost.

### 3. Scale (Exponential Resource Allocation & Automation)
Only hypotheses that survive the filter stage are funded with deep engineering, automated distribution via [[systems/social-publisher|Social Publisher]], and high-performance frontend architecture.

## Implementation in Agentic Systems

When combined with [[concepts/loop-engineering|Loop Engineering]], Fan-Filter-Scale enables an autonomous startup factory:
- Autonomous agents scan market sentiment and fan out 50+ product specs.
- Evaluator subagents run simulated critique councils to filter down to the top 2.
- The execution harness builds, tests, and deploys the landing pages instantly.
